# Research Paper

Two independent research papers on organisational behaviour, 
business ethics, and HR strategy.

---

## Mapping the Gap: A Framework for Classifying Corporate Values-Behaviour Alignment (2026)

A cross-industry multi-case study research paper examining how 
twelve global organisations relate to their stated values. 
Proposes a six-category classification framework — Consistent 
Alignment, Contextual Alignment, Strategic Drift, Opportunistic 
Drift, Symbolic Alignment, and Contradictory Behaviour — applied 
through primary source analysis across aviation, technology, 
finance, retail, EdTech, and entertainment.

Companies examined: Costco, Patagonia, Microsoft, Airbnb, 
Disney, Twitter/X, Zoom, BYJU'S, Goldman Sachs, H&M, 
Boeing, and Amazon.

(https://github.com/blaah00/Research-Paper/blob/main/Mapping%20the%20Gap%20Research%20Paper.pdf)

---

## Corporate by Design: A Forensic Examination of How Organisational Systems Shape Human Behaviour (2026)

A research paper arguing that the most consequential dysfunctions 
of contemporary work — disengagement, burnout, attrition, ethical 
drift — are not failures of culture or character but predictable 
outputs of five interlocking organisational systems: Measurement, 
Hierarchical, Economic Dependency, Psychological Conditioning, 
and Symbolic.

Examines fifteen organisations across geographies including 
McKinsey, TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, 
WeWork, Semco, Patagonia, Costco, Google, Basecamp, 
and Indian gig platforms.
